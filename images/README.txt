Drop your posters / cover art / screenshots in this folder.

Then reference them either:
  - in the site's "Edit lists" panel, in the image box:  images/breaking-bad.jpg
  - or in js/data.js, as "image": "images/breaking-bad.jpg"

You can also paste a full https://... image address instead, or use the
"Upload image" button in the editor (that copies the picture straight into
the browser's storage, so no file juggling needed).

Portrait/poster shaped images (roughly 2:3) look best. Anything works though —
images are cropped to fill the card.
